Note 1 (q6). Showing "how different parties' policies affect them" fits the site's existing deterministic layer
(the receipt grid and "What this could mean for you"). Her example, surfacing a policy as racist to a person of colour,
would mean the site making a judgement about a policy; the site's rule is to show what each party wrote and what it
would change for a household, never a verdict. The same effect within the rules: when a profile says someone is on a
visa or from an affected group, show every party's published position on immigration, equality and rights first, in
their own words. To agree with Romily.

Note 2 (q15). Ordering council items by the user's profile (their stated topics first) is allowed; ordering by what we
judge important is not. Proposal: items sorted into the user's topics first, then the rest, newest first.
