# Inviting candidates: questions for Romily

## Why this has come up

The site already says we invite candidates, but we haven't invited anyone yet.

- The page for candidates says: *"we send to every nominated candidate for whom we can find a contact address."*
- Every Holborn and St Pancras candidate's card says: *"Not yet invited to submit a statement: we have no contact address."*

Neither is true at the moment. Nobody has been invited, and Democracy Club has an email address for 9 of the 15 candidates. So we have to either start inviting candidates or change those two lines.

Two things matter here as much as fairness.

- **Bias through no fault of ours.** If only one party's candidates reply, their cards could look fuller than everyone else's, and the site would look biased.
- **Work.** The site is meant to run itself as far as possible. Anything we have to do by hand for every election won't work in May 2027, when there could be thousands of candidates.

We need an answer by **Sunday 27 September**, so that anything we choose can be in place in time for 8 October.

---

## What can run by itself and what can't

| Step | Can it be automatic? |
|---|---|
| Finding an email address | Yes, for candidates Democracy Club has one for (9 of 15 here). Anyone else is marked "no contact address found". |
| Creating each candidate's private link and sending the email | Yes. The morning update could do it the day a candidate appears. We'd need an email-sending service, which is Barny's decision. |
| Reminders, and the "invited / replied" line on each card | Yes. |
| Reading what candidates send before it goes live | **No.** Someone has to check it, because a candidate could write about an opponent, or something untrue, and it would appear on our site. |

So sending can be completely automatic. Reading replies is always work for a person, and how much work depends on what we let candidates send.

---

## Question 1: which of these do you want?

- **A. Nothing.** We don't invite anyone, and we change the two lines so they're true. Candidates can still email us if we've got something wrong. *No work for us.*
- **B. Point candidates to Democracy Club.** Democracy Club already collects "statements to voters" from candidates, and our site already shows them automatically: 12 of our 15 candidates have one. Instead of running our own invitations, we tell candidates to give their statement to Democracy Club. A new statement appears on our site the next morning, and a changed one within a week. (Barny would first check with Peter how candidates are meant to do this.) *No work for us, and every candidate uses the same route that other sites use.*
- **C. Corrections only.** We automatically invite every candidate to check their page and tell us if anything is wrong. *A little work: reading the occasional correction.*
- **D. Corrections plus short answers.** As C, but candidates can also answer the same few questions in a fixed number of words, shown in a separate labelled box. *The most work: every answer must be read before it goes live, for every election.*

If you choose A or B, you only need to answer question 12.

---

## If C or D

**2. Where should submissions appear?** Our suggestion is a small box under the card, labelled "Supplied by the candidate to What's It To Me? on [date]". It would never count in the comparison table or anywhere candidates are compared. Are you happy with that?

**3. Should every card show whether that candidate replied?** For example, "Invited 28 Sep. No reply". This shows readers that everyone had the same chance, but it also makes it obvious who didn't reply.

**4. What if only a few reply?** Do we publish replies as they come in, or wait until a set number have replied? Waiting sounds fair, but it lets the candidates who don't reply block the ones who do.

**5. Who is the sender?** For example, hello@whatsittome.org with your name on the email. The wording would be the same for everyone.

**6. Reminders.** One automatic reminder, say three days later, or none?

**7. Who reads submissions, and how quickly?** Nothing goes live until one of us has approved it.

---

## If D only

**8. Which questions?** They must be the same for everyone and must not lean any way politically. How many, and what should they be?

**9. Word limit?** We suggest about 150 words per answer.

**10. What do we refuse?** For example: anything about other candidates, personal attacks, anything over the limit, or links to fundraising pages.

**11. Do we publish their answers exactly as sent, or check them against published sources like everything else?**

---

## For every option

**12. What do we say on the site?** Whatever you choose, we need to change the two lines above. Here's suggested wording for each option.

- **A:** "We don't currently collect statements from candidates. If you are a candidate and think something on your page is wrong, email hello@whatsittome.org and we will check it against the source."
- **B:** "If you are a candidate, you can give a statement to voters to Democracy Club, and it will appear here automatically. If you think something on your page is wrong, email hello@whatsittome.org and we will check it against the source."
- **C or D:** the current wording, once it's true.
