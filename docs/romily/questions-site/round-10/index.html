Notes on Peter’s four organisations

Researched on 14 October 2026 from each organisation’s own pages. The links are the sources.

  • The Politics Project. A non-partisan democratic education organisation working with schools and youth groups across the UK: video calls between learners and elected representatives, youth hustings, and the Democracy Classroom Network of more than 100 organisations. It says it supports more than 4,000 schools and youth groups (their site), and it co-produces the UK Democracy Bulletin with Democracy Club (March 2026). Contact: info@thepoliticsproject.org.uk (contact page). The closest fit to what we do.
  • Seneca Learning. A free homework and revision platform for students from Year 3 to A level, used by about 15 million students by its own count, covering Citizenship and A-level Politics (their site, subjects). GoStudent bought it in 2022 (TechCrunch). Contact: learnmore@seneca.io. A huge student audience, but it’s a company, so expect a slower process.
  • Reach plc. The UK’s largest commercial news publisher: the Mirror, Express, Manchester Evening News, WalesOnline and the “Live” local news sites (Reach Solutions). We found no election tool of theirs. Partnership enquiries go through a form on their partnerships page. Huge local reach; best once the app and widget exist.
  • “State”. We couldn’t find a current UK politics app by that name. A website called State closed some years ago (Wikipedia), and an app called State on Google Play is a discussion app with no UK link. Worth asking Peter for a link.
