Round seven.

This one is about a single decision: whether candidates should be able to send us anything, and if so, what.

It came up because the site already says we invite candidates, and we haven't. The page for candidates says "we send to every nominated candidate for whom we can find a contact address", and every Holborn and St Pancras candidate's card says "Not yet invited to submit a statement: we have no contact address." Neither is true. Nobody has been invited, and Democracy Club has an email address for 9 of the 15 candidates. So either we start inviting, or we change those two lines.

Two worries go with it. First, if only one party's candidates reply, their cards could look fuller than everyone else's, and the site would look biased through no fault of ours. Second, the site is meant to run itself as far as possible. Anything done by hand for every election won't work in May 2027, when there could be thousands of candidates.

We need your answers by Sunday 27 September, so that whatever you choose is in place well before 8 October. None of the options is marked as ours. As before: answers save in this browser as you type, nothing is sent until you press "Send my answers", and "I don't know yet" is fine.
