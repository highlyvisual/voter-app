# Verification of review.html against the eight underlying reports and primary sources

Checked 25 September 2026 by an independent pass. Method: every number, named study, court case, date, percentage, quotation and factual claim in `bible/review.html` (sections 1–12, all 117 improvement rows) was read against `audit-technical.md`, `audit-content.md`, the five research reports and, where the reports were silent, against the raw captures in `deepdive/work/` and `deepdive/shots-content/` (page HTML, `feed.xml`, `pw-results.json`, `crawl-results.json`). Thirty-six headline figures were then re-read from their primary source by WebFetch or curl (listed in the "Source re-read" column). Nothing in review.html was edited.

Verdicts: **WRONG** = contradicted by the report or the source. **UNSUPPORTED** = not in any of the eight reports and not verifiable from the captures or the source. **IMPRECISE** = right in substance, wrong in a detail, attribution or wording that a hostile reader could use. **CONFIRMED** = matches the report and, where re-read, the source.

Totals: WRONG 10 · UNSUPPORTED 10 · IMPRECISE 22 · CONFIRMED 128 (rows; many bundle several claims).
